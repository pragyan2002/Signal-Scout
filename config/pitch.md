# Cardinal — Pitch

Cardinal is an AI outbound platform for YC-backed and seed-stage startups. We
read every prospect's recent public activity — what they post, what they're
hiring for, what tools they complain about — and write outbound that sounds
like a human who actually paid attention.

**What we replace:** generic templated cold emails that get deleted in 0.3
seconds.

**What we promise:** every outbound is grounded in something the prospect
actually said in public, in the last two weeks. No fake personalization, no
"I noticed you're a CEO." Real signals, real references, sent at human
volume not spam volume.

**The wedge:** one founder, doing their own outbound, can sound like a
five-person SDR team that did real research.
