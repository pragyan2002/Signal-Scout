# Ideal Customer Profile

**Cardinal's ICP for outbound:**

- Technical founders and early engineering leaders at YC-backed startups (or
  comparable seed/Series A companies)
- Team size: 2–30 people
- Currently doing outbound sales themselves, or have a small SDR team they're
  unhappy with
- Pain: spray-and-pray outbound doesn't convert; they know they need
  personalization but don't have time to research each prospect manually
- Buying signals: hiring SDRs/AEs, complaining about outbound tools, talking
  about pipeline, fundraising (often triggers a "grow GTM" push), launching a
  new product (need to find first customers)
